About Me

Research, Advisory & Legal Excellence Across Africa

I am an admitted attorney of the High Court of South Africa, a researcher, legal and management consultant with experience in law and regulatory compliance, learning programme design, skills development, new venture creation, taxation, debt review, business rescue, capital markets, and municipal administration.

My consulting and training experience spans more than 19 years across several countries on the African continent and the Middle East. I train and coach entrepreneurs, corporate executives, managers and policymakers in strategic management, corporate governance, law, regulatory compliance and strategic thinking for results.

Amongst several past roles, I served as the Academic Dean and Executive Director for CIDA City Campus from 2012 to 2015, and as National Vice President for Junior Chamber International (JCI) South Africa in 2013. I am a Senator of JCI (Senate No. 72025).
